LOVE LANGUAGE

> U8 · PSU2, THE FIRST OF THE SERIES, AND THE LONGEST BUILD

utsav@homelab:~/projects/love-language$ cat README.md

A quiz based on Dr. Gary Chapman's 5 Love Languages framework: words of affirmation, quality time, receiving gifts, acts of service, and physical touch. Users answer a set of scenario questions and get a scored breakdown of their primary love language, with results they can share. This was the earliest of the psychology quiz series, first built in October 2024, then substantially reworked before its public relaunch in April 2025.
